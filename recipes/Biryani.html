Biryani

Home

Description

A flavorful Pakistani chicken biryani made with rice, chicken, spices, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Basmati rice
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Biryani spices
  • Oil and salt

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice.
  2. Cook onions, chicken, tomatoes, yogurt, and spices.
  3. Partially boil the rice.
  4. Layer the rice and chicken mixture.
  5. Cook on low heat until done.
