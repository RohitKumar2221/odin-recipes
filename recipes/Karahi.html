Description

Chicken Karahi is a popular Pakistani dish made with chicken, tomatoes, green chillies, and aromatic spices. It is cooked in a karahi until the chicken becomes tender and the gravy becomes rich and flavorful.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 4 green chillies
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh ginger and coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a karahi.
  2. Add the chicken and cook until its color changes.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes, salt, and spices.
  5. Cook until the chicken is tender and the tomatoes form a thick gravy.
  6. Add green chillies and sliced ginger.
  7. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
